Before the workshop modules start, you log in to your Red Hat AI Enterprise
lab environment, get oriented in the OpenShift console and the OpenShift AI
dashboard, and verify that the platform is ready.

toc::[]

== Console URL

Your lab environment is a dedicated OpenShift cluster. Open the
link:{openshift_console_url}[OpenShift web console,window=_blank] — or use
the *OpenShift Console* tab in the workshop interface.

== Login credentials

Log in to the OpenShift console, the OpenShift AI dashboard, and the
terminal with the lab credentials provided for your environment:

[source,subs="attributes+"]
----
Username: {ssh_user}
Password: {ssh_password}
----

These credentials are scoped to your lab environment — do not share them.

== Cluster environment

Your environment runs OpenShift with Red Hat OpenShift AI (RHOAI) 3.6
installed and configured for this workshop:

* *RHOAI 3.6* — includes EvalHub for evaluations, Model-as-a-Service (MaaS)
  governance with subscription tiers and API keys, and the observability
  dashboard.
* *Your own project* — you work in your assigned project (namespace)
  throughout the workshop; other attendees cannot see or modify your
  resources.
* *GPU worker node* — an NVIDIA L40S (48 GB) GPU worker node is attached to
  the cluster and schedulable for model serving workloads.
* *No models pre-deployed* — the environment starts empty; you build the
  full serving lifecycle from scratch across the six modules.

== Navigating the console

Most workshop steps happen in the *OpenShift AI* dashboard. To reach it from
the OpenShift console, click the Application Launcher (waffle menu, in the
masthead) and select *Red Hat OpenShift AI*. You can also use the
*OpenShift AI* tab in the workshop interface. If prompted, choose
*Log in with OpenShift* and enter your lab credentials.

image::001-getting-connected-console.png[Red Hat OpenShift AI dashboard home after logging in,link=self,window=blank,width=700]

The OpenShift console itself has two perspectives, switched from the
perspective selector in the masthead:

* *Administrator* — use this perspective during the workshop; it exposes the
  cluster resources, workloads, and logs you inspect in later modules.
* *Developer* — a simplified view for application developers; it hides many
  of the resources used in this workshop.

== Verify your environment

Confirm you are connected to the cluster and that OpenShift AI is ready
before starting Module 1.

. Switch to the *Terminal* tab. If the terminal is not already
authenticated, log in with your lab credentials.
. Confirm you are logged in and working in your project:
+
[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
oc whoami
oc project
----
. Verify that the OpenShift AI platform is installed and ready — the
`DataScienceCluster` resource reports the platform status. Wait until it
reports `Ready`:
+
[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
oc get dsc
oc get dsccommon
----

[NOTE]
====
If `DataScienceCluster` is not `Ready` yet, wait a few minutes and re-run
the commands. Do not start Module 1 until the platform reports ready —
model serving depends on it.
====
