= From Model to Model as a Service: Building Scalable AI Inference
:navtitle: Lab Overview

== Welcome

In this hands-on lab you take a model through the full serving lifecycle
on Red Hat AI Enterprise — from deployment and evaluation through
Model-as-a-Service exposure, application integration, and observability.

.Duration
~2 hours (including ~10 min intro)

.Audience
AI engineers, platform engineers, and AI operations teams with basic
OpenShift/Kubernetes knowledge and familiarity with AI model concepts.

== Prerequisites

* Basic understanding of containers and Kubernetes/OpenShift
* Familiarity with generative AI model concepts (what a model is, what inference means)

== Environment

Your lab environment is a Red Hat AI Enterprise cluster. No models are
pre-deployed — you build up the full serving lifecycle from scratch.

xref:getting-connected.adoc[Start: Getting Connected →] to log in and
verify your environment before starting Module 1.
